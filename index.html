THE PROBLEM

You bid it. You do it. You lose money on it.

A job can look profitable when you quote it and become unprofitable by week 3.

  • Labour runs over your estimate.
  • Scope creeps without price adjustments.
  • Extra work gets absorbed for free.
  • Small cost overruns compound month-to-month.

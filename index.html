Why we built this

You bid a job at 25% margin. Six months in, your labor is 30% over plan. Turns out the client kept adding "just one more thing" — extra restrooms, breakroom attention, bigger trash volumes. No documentation. No change order. By the time you renew, your margin is 8%. You're stuck. Can't renegotiate without losing the contract. Can't drop the price without losing money.

That's the real problem. Not getting the bid wrong. Not knowing you got it wrong until way too late.

What ConeQuotes does

1. Calculate what to actually charge

Plug in the facility details. The calculator tells you the minimum price to hit 25% margin. You see the math before you quote.

2. Document scope so clients can't claim they didn't know

Walk the facility, take photos, generate a professional proposal. Client signs knowing exactly what you're providing. When they ask for extras later, you have proof of what was included.

3. Catch scope creep in real-time

Your crews log hours after each job. You see actual labor vs. your plan every week. If labor is drifting up, Margin Guardian alerts you before your margin disappears. You can document the scope change, send a change order, or stop it now.

How to use it

Calculator: Start here before every quote. Enter facility size, frequency, labor rate. See the price that protects your margin.

Proposal Builder: Walk the job. Take photos of each room. Enter the scope. Click "submit" to create a contract you can track.

Margin Guardian: Your contracts live here. Each week, log the hours your crews spent. Watch your real margin calculate. Get alerts if labor goes over plan.

The idea is simple:

Know your costs before you quote. Document scope so disputes don't happen. Track labor so you catch problems early. That's it. No complexity. Just the tools contractors need to protect profit.

Why we built this

Cleaning contractors manage hundreds of thousands in annual revenue, but most do it without visibility into what's actually profitable.

You quote based on experience. You execute based on habit. And you report results based on memory. That works until it doesn't — scope grows, labor drifts, margins compress. By the time you see it on a spreadsheet, you've already lost money you didn't know was disappearing.

Whether you're scaling to 50 contracts or protecting the 200 you already have, you need three things: confidence in your bids, documented scope that clients can't argue with, and real-time visibility into whether each contract is actually making money.

What ConeQuotes does

1. Calculate what to charge with confidence

Plug in facility details. The calculator shows you the price that protects your margin target. No guessing. No surprises.

2. Document scope so disputes never happen

Walk the facility. Take photos. Generate a professional proposal. Client signs knowing exactly what they're getting. When scope questions come up, you have proof.

3. Track margin in real-time

Your crews log hours after each job. You see actual labor vs. plan every week. Catch drift before it becomes a problem. Stay in control.

How to use it

Calculator: Start here before every quote. Enter facility size, frequency, labor rate. See the price that protects your margin.

Proposal Builder: Walk the job. Take photos of each room. Enter the scope. Click "submit" to create a contract you can track.

Margin Guardian: Your contracts live here. Each week, log the hours your crews spent. Watch your real margin calculate. Get alerts if labor goes over plan.

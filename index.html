The cleaning contract problem—and how ConeQuotes fixes it

1

Vague scope = scope creep

You quote "general cleaning" at a rate per square foot. Within three months, restrooms need daily attention, breakroom gets extra attention, trash volumes increase. Nowhere to document it. No change order. Extra labor = lost profit.

✓ ConeQuotes way

You capture a detailed scope—restrooms, breakroom, lobbies, trash, frequency. A crew or client requests extra work. You log it (photo + estimate), and it's documented for a change order. No unbilled extras.

2

No profit visibility

You send a proposal and think you're at 25% margin. Six months in, actual labor is 30% over plan. You discover it too late when the renewal comes. Margin is now 8%. Too late to fix it.

✓ ConeQuotes way

Every month, you see planned versus actual labor. If hours exceed scope, you get an alert: "Restroom work is 18 hours over plan." You submit a change order, or adjust next month's crew plan. Margin stays protected.

3

Clients question service

"Was cleaning done last Tuesday?" You have no proof beyond a crew member's word. Dispute, churn, reputation damage.

✓ ConeQuotes way

After every shift, a supervisor logs arrival, completed checklist, any issues, optional photos. Client sees it in a branded portal. Zero disputes. Renewal is easy because the data proves value.
